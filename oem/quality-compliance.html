OEM · Quality

Quality you can audit

Reviews are written by the hundredth pad you ship, not the first. Our controls are designed to make every carton match the approved specification.

Incoming

Fabric GSM, width, colour against the standard, TPU peel strength.

Cutting

Dimension checks on the first piece of each lay.

In-line

Stitch density, quilting alignment, binding coverage.

Final

AQL 2.5 / 4.0, carton weight check, needle detection.

Lab

Hydrostatic, absorbency and wash-cycle tests per lot.
