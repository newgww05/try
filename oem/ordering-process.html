OEM · Ordering

From first email to delivered cartons

A clear, predictable process with named milestones. Most private-label projects reach the first shipment in 6–9 weeks.

StageWhat happensTypical time
1. RFQYou share the market, sizes, grade, quantity and packaging. We send a spec sheet and price ladder.1 business day
2. SamplesStock samples, or custom samples with your logo and fabric3–5 / 7–12 days
3. Testing & approvalOptional third-party tests, packaging artwork, golden sample sealed5–10 days
4. Deposit & productionMaterial booking, cutting, quilting, sewing, packing25–35 days
5. InspectionOur QC, or your third party (SGS, Intertek, QIMA, etc.)1–2 days
6. ShippingFOB / CIF / DDP; sea, rail or air; FBA prepRoute-dependent

MOQ

Stock build: 500 pcs per size. Custom: 1,000 pcs per SKU. A mixed first order is possible.

Payment

30% deposit, 70% against B/L copy (T/T). L/C at sight for larger orders.

Samples

Stock samples are free; you pay the courier. Custom sample fees are credited against your first order.

Times and terms are typical and confirmed on each quotation.

Ready to build your washable underpad line?

Tell us your market, sizes and quantities. We reply with a spec sheet, price ladder and sample plan within one business day.
