What it means

Takeaways for a pet brand

  • Growth comes from urban living, apartment dogs, and repeat online purchasing
  • Washable pads win on waste and staying power; the common objection is the higher first price
  • Review sites reward fast drying, no shrinkage and true waterproofing, and punish chewing damage and odour
  • Crate-matched sizes and senior-pet positioning are less crowded than generic puppy pads
  • In Japan, disposable ペットシーツ dominate; a quality washable pad is a clear niche. To protect floors and furniture, owners still need a large, thick, durable washable pad.
