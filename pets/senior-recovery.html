Pet Pads · Senior & medical

Senior, incontinent & recovering pets

An older dog that leaks in its sleep, a spayed female with weak bladder control, a cat recovering from surgery: these pets need dry bedding night after night, and their owners need protection that is easy to wash.

Veterinary facts

Incontinence is common, and often treatable

5.1%Of 566 spayed female dogs developed acquired urinary incontinenceJAVMA, 2013
3–20%Reported prevalence in spayed female dogs across studiesACVIM consensus
≈ 7×Higher risk for large dogs than for small dogsJAVMA, 2013
2.3 yrsAverage time from spaying to onset in that studyJAVMA, 2013
A pad manages the mess; it does not treat the cause. Sudden accidents, straining, blood in the urine or a strong smell can be signs of infection or other illness. Owners should see a vet.
What owners need

Their daily reality

  • Dry bedding through the night, every night
  • Protection for sofas and beds where older pets like to sleep
  • Pads that wash quickly, because they are needed again tomorrow
  • Changing wet pads promptly, to keep the pet's skin clean and dry
  • For cats: a pad around the litter box for misses and scatter
