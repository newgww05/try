Pet Pads · Home & travel

Crates, sofas & cars

Wherever a pet sleeps, rides or plays, a washable pad keeps the surface underneath clean. Here is how to pick the right size for each place.

6
Crate-size chart

Match the pad to the crate

Crate sizes are named by length. Floor dimensions vary by maker, so measure the floor before you order.

Crate lengthSize classTypical floor (approx.)VELCURA pad
24 inSmall24 × 18 in18 × 24 in

Size classes based on PetSmart's crate chart, which matches small crates to dogs of about 11–25 lb and XL crates to dogs of about 71–90 lb. Measure the dog too: length plus about 4 in. For brands: crate-matched pad sizes are an easy way to stand out on marketplaces.

Around the home

Where else pads go

Sofas & beds

36 × 60 in pads cover a sofa seat or the foot of a bed where pets sleep.

Car trunks

Wet or muddy dogs after a walk. See our car trunk page.

Floors & feeding areas

By the door, under water bowls and in play pens, with anti-slip dots on tiles and wood.

Litter areas

18 × 24 in pads around the litter box catch scatter and misses.

Whelping boxes

Heavy-grade pads under litters, changed and washed several times a day.

Travel & holidays
