Pet Pads · Care

Washing pet pads

Urine odour is the number-one complaint about reusable pet pads. Most of it comes down to washing order: rinse cold first, then wash warm, and never use fabric softener.

Shake off

Remove solids and hair outdoors or into the bin.

Rinse cold

Rinse in cold water first. Hot water sets the proteins in urine.

Pre-soak (optional)

For strong odour, soak for 2–4 hours in a baking-soda solution.

Wash warm

40–60 °C, with an enzyme laundry additive, on its own or with other pet textiles.

No softener

Fabric softener coats the fibres and cuts absorbency. No chlorine bleach on coloured pads.

Dry

Tumble dry on low, or line-dry in the sun, which also helps freshen the pad.

Routine

How often, how many

  • In daily use, wash every 24 hours
  • Keep 3–4 pads in rotation per pet
  • Wash pet pads separately from family bedding
  • If a pad still smells, rinse and wash a second time before using it
Health check

When accidents mean a vet visit

More frequent accidents, straining, pain, blood in the urine or a strong smell can be signs of a urinary infection or other illness. A pad keeps the home clean; a vet finds the cause.
