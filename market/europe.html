Markets · Europe

Europe: care-aid systems and 95 °C

European buyers expect hot-wash durability, textile labelling compliance and sustainability credentials. In Germany, reusable bed-protection pads are also part of the statutory care-aid system.

Germany: care-aid listing

Reusable absorbent bed-protection pads (wiederverwendbare saugende Bettschutzeinlagen) are listed in the statutory aids directory (product group 51) and can be supplied through the long-term care insurance (Pflegekasse) to people with a recognised care level. That gives distributors a reimbursed channel next to retail.

95 °C expectation

German bestseller lists for waschbare Inkontinenzunterlage (for example Pataya 75×90 cm 2-pack, Sensalou, Barbons) highlight washing at up to 95 °C and tumble-dryer safety. Kaufland ratings are typically 4.6–4.7★.

Sustainability pull

EU waste policy, green-claims rules and consumer preference for reusables favour washable pads. Claims need evidence, so the LCA and test data matter.
