10 specs that decide your reviews

Two pads that look identical in a photo can perform very differently after 30 washes. Put these ten lines in your purchase specification.

#SpecAsk forRed flag
1Barrier typeLaminated TPU film"Waterproof coating" with no detail; PVC
2Core weightg/m² stated per gradeOnly "super absorbent"
3Capacity testMethod plus value in mlCapacity with no method
4Wash ratingCycles, temperature, retest results"Washable" only
5Dimensional change≤ 3–5% after 5 washesNo data; cotton-only top on a large pad
6Quilt spacing≤ 6 cmWide, decorative quilting
7Edge finishDouble-stitched bindingOverlock-only edges
8Backing gripSilicone dots or wings for the use caseSlippery backing for sofa / dementia use
9Fabric safetyOEKO-TEX® certificate number and scopeA logo on the box with no certificate
10Packing accuracyCarton weight check, AQL levelNo final inspection
