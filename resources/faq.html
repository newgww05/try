Resources · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

For caregivers, parents and professional buyers. Can't find your question? Ask us.

What is a washable underpad?

A reusable, flat, quilted pad with an absorbent core and a waterproof backing. It is placed on a bed, chair or floor to protect against urine, other fluids and spills. It is also called a bed pad, chux, incontinence pad or mattress protector pad.

What is the fundamental difference from disposable underpads?

A washable underpad can be machine-washed 300+ times, so its total cost per use is about 73% lower than a disposable pad, including laundry costs. The more fundamental difference is protection: it does not shift and does not leak at the edges, so it keeps the mattress from being soiled or even ruined. That makes the overall value higher still.

How much can a washable underpad absorb?

It depends on size and core weight. A comparable 34×36 in standard pad is published at about 7 oz (≈200 ml). VELCURA offers Light, Standard and Heavy grades, and the certified value is stated on each lot's test report.

How many times can it be washed?

The standard build is rated for 300 home-laundry cycles. Our Care-Pro 95 build is designed for institutional 95 °C laundering.

Is it noisy like a plastic sheet?

No. Our standard pads use a breathable TPU membrane, so they are quiet and do not trap heat.

Will it feel hot or uncomfortable to sleep on?

No. The quilted top is soft brushed polyester or cotton, and the TPU barrier lets water vapour through, unlike the plastic backing of a disposable pad or a vinyl sheet. The core draws liquid inwards so the surface does not stay wet, and the quilting (wave or diamond) keeps the core from bunching when the user turns over.

Which side goes up?

The soft quilted side faces up, towards the person. The smooth or anti-slip side faces the mattress. Two-tone versions make this obvious.

Should it go on top of the sheet or under it?

Either works. On top of the sheet is easiest to change and check, and suits night-time care and children. Under the fitted sheet is more discreet and suits long-term bed rest or people sensitive to texture, but liquid passes through the sheet first, so the sheet needs changing too. Either way, on anti-slip versions the silicone-dot side faces the mattress.

Can it be used for babies?

Yes, on changing tables and under the fitted crib sheet. The standard top is soft brushed polyester; our Bamboo (VC-PB01) and Cotton surface (VC-PC01) styles offer softer tops, with OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 product class I fabric options, and the certificate number is provided with your order. For newborns, lay it flat, never stack pads, and keep the sleep space free of loose textiles; an underpad is not a sleep surface on its own.

Can I use bleach or fabric softener?

Use oxygen bleach if needed. Chlorine bleach should only be used, diluted, on white pads. Avoid fabric softener, because it reduces absorbency.

Why does absorbency drop after a few washes?

In most cases it is residue from fabric softener or silicone-containing detergent, which clogs the gaps between the fibres. Stop using softener, dose detergent normally and, if needed, run one extra warm-water rinse; absorbency usually comes back. Avoid chlorine bleach (except diluted on white pads) and never iron the waterproof side, because heat and chlorine can damage the TPU membrane.

Do you offer private label?

Yes. We cover sizes, fabrics, colours, logos and packaging. Custom MOQs typically start at 1,000 pieces per SKU.

Are your products medical devices?

They are sold as general care textiles. If you plan medical claims, talk to us first; the regulatory route differs by market.

Can you ship to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We supply FNSKU-labelled, polybagged units and compliant carton labels, and work with your forwarder for US, EU and JP FBA delivery.

What certificates do you have?

OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certificates for the fabrics used, plus REACH, CPSIA and formaldehyde test reports by market. Certificate numbers and scopes are shared with quotations.
