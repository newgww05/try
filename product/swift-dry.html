Swift Dry style underpad

For high-turnover laundry programmes. A loop-pile top over a heavy 340 g/m² core (≈ 400 ml per 34 × 36 in pad). Strike-through and drying times are measured for your order and stated on the test report.

21
4-layer protection structure

What it is made of

  1. TopLoop-pile fabric 150 g/m²
  2. Absorbent core65% polyester / 35% viscose, 340 g/m²; ≈ 400 ml per 34 × 36 in pad
  3. Waterproof barrierTPU film 0.025 mm, 30 g/m²; machine washable 300+ times
  4. BackingSoft brushed polyester 90 g/m², anti-slip
