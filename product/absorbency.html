Product · Performance

Absorbency you can put on a spec sheet

"Super absorbent" means little to a buyer. We publish grade targets, test methods and wash-durability data, so you can compare pads on facts.

Grades

Three absorbency grades

Capacity grows with core weight and pad area. Figures are for a 34 × 36 in (86 × 91 cm) pad.

Light · 200 g/m²

≈ 200 ml / 7 oz

Furniture, travel, baby changing, light bladder leaks. Dries fastest.

Standard · 300 g/m²

≈ 300 ml / 10 oz

Nightly bed use, bedwetting children, senior pets. Best value.

Heavy · 400 g/m² +

≈ 500 ml / 17 oz

Overnight heavy incontinence, bed-bound care, institutional laundry.

About these numbers. The 7 oz figure is the published capacity of comparable retail 34×36 in pads. Standard and Heavy values are VELCURA design targets. The certified value for your order appears on the third-party test report issued before mass production. We never print an unverified capacity on your packaging.

Durability

Why "300 washes" is credible

Most washable pads fail at the barrier, not the core: a thin PU coating cracks after repeated hot drying. A laminated TPU film is bonded to knit fabric, so it bends with the pad instead of cracking.

We re-test hydrostatic pressure after 50, 100 and 300 ISO 6330 cycles. A lot is released only if it still passes after the final cycle.
