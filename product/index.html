The VELCURA washable underpad

A flat, quilted, four-layer pad that goes on top of the sheet, under the person, pet or child. It absorbs, contains and washes clean, hundreds of times.

Standard configuration

Top layerQuilted, wave or diamond pattern; 55% cotton / 45% polyester (standard)
Absorbent coreNeedle-punched polyester / viscose, 200 / 300 / 400 g/m²
BarrierTPU laminate, PVC-free, breathable
BackingPolyester knit; optional silicone anti-slip dots
