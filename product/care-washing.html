Washing & care

Treat it like a good towel, not like a shirt. A few simple rules keep a pad soft, absorbent and waterproof for hundreds of cycles.

Wash

Machine wash warm, 30–60 °C (90–140 °F). Care-Pro 95 build: up to 95 °C.

Dry

Tumble dry low–medium, 50–70 °C (120–160 °F), or line dry in the shade.

Detergent

Any mild detergent. Use oxygen bleach if needed. No chlorine bleach on coloured pads.

Avoid

No fabric softener, which coats fibres and cuts absorbency. No ironing of the waterproof side.

Home routine

The three-pad rotation

For nightly use, a household needs at least three pads: one on the bed, one in the wash and one dry spare. Four is better when the dryer is shared or pads are line-dried.

  • Rinse heavily soiled pads in cold water first, because hot water sets proteins
  • Wash with towels, not with zippers or velcro
  • Let pads dry completely before storing them folded
  • Before the first use, wash once to fluff up the core
Institutional laundry

For care homes and hospitals

Facilities using thermal disinfection programmes (for example 71 °C for 3 min, or 80–90 °C cycles) should specify the Care-Pro 95 build with a polyester-rich top and high-temperature TPU.
