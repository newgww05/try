Sizes & specifications

Twelve standard formats, up to 34 × 76 in (86 × 193 cm), that match how US, European and Japanese shoppers search and how their beds and chairs are sized. Custom sizes are cut to order.

Colours & packing

Stock colours

White Light blue Mint Grey Navy

Two-tone builds (a white top with a coloured back) show at a glance which side faces up. Dark colours hide stains, which pet and adult-care buyers often prefer.

Standard packing

  • Folded, packed in a PE / recycled-PE bag with a header card, or in a colour box
  • Retail packs of 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6
  • Export carton 5-ply, 20–40 pcs depending on size
  • Amazon FNSKU labels and suffocation warnings on request
  • Approx. pad weight: 34×36 in, Standard grade ≈ 330–380 g
