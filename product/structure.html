Product · Structure

Four layers, one quiet job

Every layer has one task: keep skin dry, hold liquid, block it, or keep the pad in place. Here is how each one works and which options you can choose.

Cross-section

How liquid travels

Liquid passes quickly through the open quilted top, spreads sideways along the quilting channels and is held in the core's fibre matrix. The TPU film stops anything reaching the mattress. Water vapour still passes through the film, so the bed does not get hot and clammy.

Quilting is a functional choice, not decoration. Dense wave stitching keeps the core from bunching after hundreds of washes and spreads liquid over a larger area, so it soaks in faster.

Layer by layer

Materials and options

① Quilted top layer

Standard: 55% cotton / 45% polyester, wave quilting. Options: 100% cotton, bamboo viscose (very soft), polyester knit (fast drying, pet-proof), diamond or channel quilting. Moves moisture away from the skin quickly.

② Absorbent core

Needle-punched polyester / viscose batting in three weights: 200 g/m² (Light), 300 g/m² (Standard), 400 g/m² (Heavy). An optional second core layer is available for overnight heavy use.

③ Waterproof barrier

Thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) film laminated to knit fabric. It is 100% liquid-proof, breathable, quiet, and contains no PVC or phthalate plasticisers. It is rated for repeated hot washes, unlike a thin acrylic or PU coating.

④ Backing

Smooth polyester for beds with fitted sheets. Silicone anti-slip dots for leather sofas, wooden floors and pet crates. Tuck-in wings (2 × 46 × 86 cm / 18 × 34 in) for mattresses.

Finishing

Details that decide the 300th wash

  • Bias-tape binding, double-stitched on all four edges
  • Rounded corners to stop "dog ears" forming in the dryer
  • Quilting spaced 4–6 cm apart, so the core cannot migrate
  • Woven or printed care label, positioned away from the skin side
  • Optional colour-contrast backing, so the correct side faces up at a glance
