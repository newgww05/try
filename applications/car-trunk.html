Application

Car trunk

After the dog walk, the beach or the garden centre, the car trunk takes the mess. A washable pad lines the cargo floor, catches every drip and goes straight into the washing machine at home.

What users need

Their daily reality

  • Wet, sandy or muddy dogs jumping in after a walk
  • Carpet that is hard to clean and keeps odours
  • Leaking groceries, plants, compost bags and cool boxes
  • Wet wetsuits, swimwear, boots and sports kit
  • A pad that stays put when bags slide around on corners
  • Quick removal: shake out, wash, put back
Recommended spec

VELCURA build

GradeStandard: catches drips and paw-wet fur
Size34×52 in for hatchbacks, 36×60 in for SUV and estate trunks
TopTight polyester knit, which resists claws and releases sand and hair
BackingSilicone anti-slip dots so it does not slide on trunk carpet
ColourGrey or navy, which hide dirt
ExtrasOptional bumper flap and corner straps (custom)
Why a washable pad

Better than a moulded boot liner?

  • Absorbs instead of pooling: water and drool soak into the quilted core rather than sloshing around a plastic tray
  • Machine washable: rubber and PVC trunk trays have to be hosed down and do not fit in a washer
  • Soft and quiet for dogs that lie down on long drives
  • Universal fit: one size covers many cars, and it doubles as a back-seat or crate pad
  • Folds flat under the trunk floor when you are not using it
Safety: a pad protects the car, not the passenger. Secure dogs with a crash-tested crate, harness or dog guard as local rules require.
Product idea for brands

A "car kit" bundle

For pet and outdoor retailers: one 36×60 in trunk pad, one 18×24 in seat pad and a drawstring wash bag in a single box. It is easy to gift and lifts the basket value.
