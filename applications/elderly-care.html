Application

Elderly & home care

Caring for an ageing parent at home means many small, repeated tasks. A dependable bed pad turns a night-time accident into a two-minute change instead of a full remake of the bed.

What users need

Their daily reality

  • Protect the mattress without a noisy, hot plastic sheet
  • Change the pad without changing all the bedding, sometimes with the person still in bed (log-roll)
  • Keep the pad in place despite restless movement
  • Skin comfort for long hours in bed
  • Laundry-friendly; often several pads a week
Recommended spec

VELCURA build

GradeStandard (light-moderate) or Heavy (overnight)
Size34×52 in; 34×76 in for head-to-toe cover; 34×36 or 34×52 in with 46×86 cm tuck-in wings
TopCotton/poly quilted, soft and quick-wicking
BackingSilicone anti-slip, or wings
ColourWhite / light blue (looks clinical-clean) or navy (hides stains)
Pack2- or 4-pack for rotation
Caregiver tips

Making changes easier

Place the pad under the hips, between the fitted sheet and the person. For a bed-bound person, roll them to one side, fold the soiled pad inwards, slide in a fresh pad folded in half, roll them back and unfold.

A pad with 46 × 86 cm (18 × 34 in) wings tucked under the mattress stays in place better for people living with dementia or restlessness. Use a lift-sheet-rated product, not an underpad, to reposition a person.

Also for

Postpartum & heavy-flow nights

After childbirth and during heavy menstrual nights, a soft 34×36 in pad protects the mattress without a crinkly feel. Dark colours (navy or grey) are preferred here.
