Application

Outdoor & camping

The same layer that keeps a mattress dry works in reverse outdoors. With the waterproof side down, it blocks damp grass and sand. Quilted side up, it is a soft, clean place to sit, sleep or change.

What users need

Their daily reality

  • A dry seat on dewy grass, damp benches and stadium seats
  • Protecting sleeping bags and air mattresses for children who still wet the bed at night, or older adults on camping trips
  • Clean changing spot for babies away from home
  • Something that packs small and survives sand, mud and the washing machine
  • Comfort: softer than a thin foil or plastic picnic sheet
Recommended spec

VELCURA build

GradeLight (seat, picnic) or Standard (overnight sleeping-bag protection)
Size36×60 in picnic and tent, 24×36 in seat and changing
TopPolyester knit, quick drying, releases sand
BackingSmooth TPU side faces the ground
ColourMint, navy and grey; outdoor prints on request
ExtrasRoll-up strap with carry handle, or a stuff sack (custom)
Use cases

Where it goes

  • Picnic and park: a dry, soft layer over wet grass
  • Camping: under a child's sleeping bag, so a night-time accident does not ruin the trip or the bag
  • Beach and lake: sand shakes off the knit top, and it washes clean at home
  • Stadium, bleachers and festivals: a folded 24×36 in pad is a dry, cushioned seat
  • Road trips and caravans: changing station, seat protector and bed pad in one
Note for listings: describe it as a "waterproof mat" or "picnic pad" rather than a groundsheet. It is a textile pad, not a tent footprint, and should not be left on sharp rocks or near open fire.
