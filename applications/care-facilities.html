Application

Care facilities

For institutions, reusable underpads are a laundry programme rather than a product: durable builds, predictable cycles and measurably less waste than disposables.

What users need

Their daily reality

  • Survive thermal-disinfection wash cycles
  • Consistent sizing for standard care beds
  • Colour or label coding by ward or size
  • Supply continuity and stable unit cost
  • Documented infection-control protocol
