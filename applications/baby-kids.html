Application

Baby & kids

From the first nappy change to the last dry night, a soft washable pad saves mattresses and laundry piles, and keeps up with a growing family.

3
What users need

Their daily reality

  • Soft, skin-safe fabric against delicate skin
  • Quick changes in the middle of the night
  • Protection during nappy-free time and potty training
  • Bedwetting support for older children (it is common up to around age 7)
  • Portable for grandparents' houses and travel

Safety first

Notes for baby-category buyers

  • Underpads are not a sleep surface. Place them under a fitted crib sheet or use them for supervised changing only
  • No loose ties, strings or small parts
  • Packaging carries polybag suffocation warnings in the market language
  • Third-party tests for formaldehyde, pH, AZO dyes and heavy metals are available on request
