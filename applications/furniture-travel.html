Application

Furniture, wheelchair & travel

Accidents do not only happen in bed. Small anti-slip pads give discreet protection on the sofa, in the wheelchair, in the car and away from home.

What users need

Their daily reality

  • Discreet, so it looks like a throw and not a medical product
  • Stays put on leather and fabric
  • Small enough to fold into a bag
  • Protects upholstery that cannot be washed
Recommended spec

VELCURA build

GradeLight / Standard
Size18×24 in (seat), 24×36 in (recliner)
TopCotton blend in a home-décor colour
BackingSilicone anti-slip
ExtraOptional carry pouch for travel sets
Hospitality

Hotels & holiday rentals

Hotels can keep a few pads in housekeeping for guests travelling with children or older relatives. That protects mattresses that cost far more than the pads, and the pads go through the normal linen laundry.
